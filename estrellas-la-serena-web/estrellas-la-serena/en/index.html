    04 / Cloud policy

    What if it's cloudy?

    It's what you ask us most, so here it is: we never sell a cloudy night as a good one. Roughly one night in six is cancelled because of clouds.

    1. We decide on the day

    Elena checks the forecast on the day and, before 5 pm, lets you know by email (and phone if you gave us one).

    2. You choose

    Move to another available night or cancel. As nothing is paid when booking, there's nothing to refund or claim.

    3. If it's borderline, we tell you

    If the forecast is uncertain, we tell you what you'll be able to see and you decide whether to come.

    05 / Groups

    Groups & companies: weeknight skies for everyone

    Team dinners, associations or groups of friends, especially Monday to Thursday, when the sky is quietest. So that everyone gets a good look through the telescope, never more than 15 people stargaze at once: large groups have dinner together and stargaze in shifts.

    First
    The whole group has dinner together in the countryside, with local produce.
    Then
    The first shift (up to 15) stargazes with Elena while the rest enjoy the after-dinner chat.
    Finally
    Shifts swap. Nobody misses the telescope and nobody waits standing up.

    06 / Getting there

    Getting there

    We meet at a point in the Castuera area (Badajoz), away from village lights. The exact location comes in your confirmation email with a map link.

    Area: Castuera, La Serena district (Badajoz, Extremadura, Spain).

    See the area on the map

    You'll need a car. There's parking next to the meeting point.

    Meeting time: 1 hour after dark (around 7:30 pm in winter, around 11 pm in summer). We'll confirm it by email.

    What to bring: warm clothes (nights are cool even in summer), comfortable shoes and a red-light torch if you have one.

    Children: welcome with an adult. We recommend it from about age 6 as it ends late.

    07 / FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    What happens if it's cloudy?

    We don't go out. We let you know before 5 pm on the day and you choose another night or cancel without paying anything.

    Do you run sessions for groups and companies?

    Yes. Up to 15 people stargaze at once; large groups have dinner together and stargaze in shifts. Request a proposal.

    How do I get there?

    By car, to the Castuera area (Badajoz). The exact point is in your confirmation email.

    Can children come?

    Yes, with an adult. Recommended from about age 6.

    Is it in English?

    Yes. Elena guides in English and Spanish: choose the language when booking.

    Why no full moon sessions?

    Because moonlight hides most of the deep sky. We'd rather not sell you a night that isn't worth it.
