Privacy policy

Data controller: Estrellas de La Serena (Elena and Miguel), Castuera (Badajoz, Spain). Contact: hola@estrellasdelaserena.es.

What we ask for and why

  • Bookings: name, email, number of people, language and, optionally, mobile. Used to confirm your booking, send you the meeting point and tell you if the session is cancelled because of clouds.
  • Groups & companies: company or group name, email, number of people and approximate dates. Only to prepare the proposal.
  • Question assistant: what you type is not stored. If you choose to write to Elena, your email app opens and you decide whether to send it.

We don't ask for payment details, date of birth or address. We never use your data for advertising or share it.

Legal basis

Your consent when ticking the box and the booking you ask us to manage (GDPR art. 6.1.a and 6.1.b).

How long we keep it

Up to one month after the session, to handle changes or questions. Then it's deleted. Group requests that don't go ahead are deleted after three months.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to hola@estrellasdelaserena.es. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Cookies

This site uses no advertising or analytics cookies. It only stores your booking in your own browser's storage.

Class case study: fictional business. In this demo, bookings are stored only in the booker's browser and are not sent to any server.
